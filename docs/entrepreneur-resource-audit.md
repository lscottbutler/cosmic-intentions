Pricing uses YC's library video metadata; leadership uses the specific lecture link in YC's week-10 article; sales uses the How to Sell iframe on YC's Tyler Bosmeny page. Related-post embeds are not valid evidence for the main lesson.

Podcast URLs open specific publisher episode pages with listening choices. Mel audio uses a suggested 10-minute session, without assuming video and audio ad placements match. YC recap timings refer only to the separately edited audio. Reading sessions are suggested time budgets, not measured article durations.

Article titles/content were checked on author/publisher pages through direct retrieval or web indexing. Some publishers block automated direct requests, so this is source verification rather than a guarantee of HTTP availability for every member. Medium and other publishers may introduce sign-in or subscription prompts.

Videos remain on their original platforms. No video/audio files, transcripts, or third-party images were copied into the repository. CI takeaways are short paraphrases; exercises are original applications, not speaker quotations. YouTube loads after a play/chapter click. Its standard controls and timestamped external fallback stay available.

Live third-party playback is unverified: the review link is password protected and the available cloud browser previously received a YouTube unusual-traffic challenge. Browser tests use a labelled YouTube API fixture to verify video selection, seek, pause, save and replay; they do not prove actual streaming, regional availability or embedding permission.
