## The 5 rules that matter for the CI landing page

1. **Sell the result, not the feature.** Every visitor is silently asking "what's in
   it for me?" Lead with the transformation (who they *become*), not the mechanism
   ("operating system for life design" = feature = weak).

2. **Name one big problem.** Melissa's pain: she's "all talk," another year slips by,
   she's quit apps before. Speak to that, then offer the fix.

3. **The "so that" bullet.** feature → *so that* → emotional payoff.
   e.g. "Hear your goals on loop **so that** your subconscious believes them before
   your doubts wake up."

4. **Reverse the risk.** Make starting feel safe and tiny: "7 days free · no charge
   today · cancel in two taps."

5. **One dead-clear call to action.** Tell them exactly what to do next, benefit-loaded:
   "Start free — your first 'I AM' takes 60 seconds."

## CI rewrites (current working set)

**Hook line (above the headline — the belief we're selling):**
> You don't need more motivation. You need to hear the truth about who you are —
> often enough to finally believe it.

**Headline options:**
- A: **Stop *setting* goals. Start *becoming* them.**  ← recommended
- B: Your goals, in your own voice, on repeat — until they're simply who you are.
- C: Become the person who already has it — in 5 minutes a day.

**Subhead:**
> Cosmic Intentions turns your "I AM" goals into an AI roadmap you can actually
> follow — then loops them back in a calm voice while you visualize, meditate, and sleep.

**"So that" bullets:**
- Hear your goals in a calm voice on loop — so that your subconscious believes them
  before your doubts wake up.
- Get a 10-step AI roadmap for any goal — so that "I don't know where to start" stops
  being the thing that stops you.
- Close 3 simple rings a day — so that you can *see*, in black and white, that you're
  becoming the person.

**CTA + risk reversal:**
> **Start free — your first "I AM" takes 60 seconds**
> 7 days free · no charge today · cancel in two taps.
