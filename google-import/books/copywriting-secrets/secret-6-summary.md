# Copywriting Secrets — Secret 6: The Single Most Important Piece of Sales Copy Ever

Source: user-pasted chapter (David Ogilvy quote, headlines are the most important skill)

## One-sentence summary
A headline is the most powerful sales asset: it must stop the reader, connect emotionally with the right prospect, and be tested ruthlessly because the entire sales process depends on it.

## Key points
- More people read headlines than body copy; a strong headline is often the difference between zero sales and a profitable campaign.
- A great headline can make mediocre body copy outperform excellent copy with a weak headline.
- Headlines must connect emotionally to fear or desire and target your ideal audience, not just attract any click.
- Poor headlines waste time, energy, and money because the right people never read the message.

## Story example
- The author had a product that got traffic but no sales. A headline change alone produced the first sale and then five sales, turning a failing project into a six-figure product in seven days.
- The original headline was self-focused and off-putting; the final headline emphasized emotional benefit and an "unfair advantage." That shift connected with buyers.

## Practical rules
- Always think of titles, video names, blog post headlines, and social post openers as headlines.
- Use headline formulas and a swipe file to generate options quickly.
- Spend serious time on the headline; it should not be an afterthought.
- Test multiple headlines against each other and keep the highest-performing control.

## CI copy action points
1. Treat every title as a headline and spend focused time improving it.
2. Build a swipe file of attention-grabbing headlines and adapt their formulas.
3. Emphasize emotional benefits and the ideal prospect in each headline.
4. Test headlines relentlessly and iterate from the top performer.
