## One-sentence summary
Headlines are the single most important copywriting skill — they must stop attention, connect emotionally (fear or desire), and target the ideal prospect; invest time, use templates and swipe files, and always test.

## Key points
- Headlines determine whether people read the rest of your message; a strong headline can make mediocre body copy outperform great copy with a weak headline.
- Headlines must connect emotionally (appeal to a fear or a desire) and target your perfect prospect to improve click quality.
- A great headline can reduce overall clicks but increase conversion quality — better traffic is worth fewer clicks.
- Consequences of weak headlines: wasted effort, poor results, frustration, and lost opportunities.

## Illustrative story (short)
- Author launched a product that initially had zero sales despite traffic. Changing the headline from a self-focused, off-putting line to a benefit-driven, emotionally charged headline produced the first sales and rapidly scaled revenue.

## Practical rules and tactics
- Always use a headline (titles, video titles, email teasers, social posts are headlines).
- Build and maintain a swipe file of headlines and ads that grab attention; adapt formulas you find in magazines, mailers, and other ads.
- Spend meaningful time on headlines — often up to 50% of the time for the whole piece.
- Test headlines against each other; keep a winning control and try to improve it.

## Headline templates to use immediately
- How To Get ____
- How To ____ In As Little As ____ (optionally: "... even if you ____")
- How Every ____ Can ____
- "N Quick And Easy Ways To ____" (use odd numbers: 3,5,7)
- Mistakes headlines: "Which Of These ____ Mistakes Will You Make?"
- Warning headlines: "WARNING: Here’s What Every ____ Needs To Know About ____"
- Solution/Proven method: "Here’s The Perfect Solution If You Want ____"; "My Proven ____ Method To ____"

## Actionable checklist for CI copy
1. Create a swipe file of 20–50 headlines that resonate with our target audience and extract templates.  
2. For each landing headline, write 6 variants: 3 curiosity, 2 benefit, 1 warning.  
3. A/B test headlines and keep the best-performing control.  
4. Ensure headlines target emotional benefit or fear and match the landing page offer (avoid misleading hype).  
5. Allocate focused time to headline iteration (don’t treat it as an afterthought).
