The daily loop

Five small steps, one goal at a time

Not a content library. A loop you live each day.

  1. Set

    Write your goal as an “I AM” intention, in your own words.

  2. Plan

    AI drafts a roadmap. Break any step into smaller ones when you need more detail.

  3. Rewire

    Hear your own words read back in a calm voice, and see your goal in visualization.

  4. Practice

    Close your daily rings: review, visualize, meditate.

  5. Reflect

    End the day with a short look back at what moved.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What happens on day 14?

Your free trial ends. If you haven't cancelled, your $79 annual membership starts and you're charged that day. Cancel before then and you won't be charged.

Is my information private?

Your goals, notes and reflections belong to your account and aren't shown to other members. To create your audio and roadmaps, only the text that feature needs is sent to the AI services that generate it. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Is this therapy?

No. Cosmic Intentions is a personal-development tool for goals and daily practice. It isn't therapy or medical care and doesn't replace a qualified professional. If you're struggling, please reach out to a licensed professional or a local support service.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your account. Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing. Cancel after you've been charged and your membership stays active until the end of your paid year, and you can still ask for a full refund within 14 days of the charge.

Whose voice is it?

The words are yours; the voice isn't. The Mind Loop reads your own words in a calm, natural-sounding AI narrator voice. It doesn't copy or clone anyone's voice, including yours. If the narrator isn't available, your device's built-in voice reads them instead.
