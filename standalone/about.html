About Us

At Cosmic Intentions, we believe personal development is personal. And beneath that sits the one idea everything we build rests on: human beings are creative beings. It is the thing our species does — we imagine something that isn't there yet, and then we make it real.

Everything ever made by human hands began as a thought. The chair you're sitting in, the device in your hand, the building around you — each one existed as an idea in someone's mind before it existed in the world. Thought is the first draft of everything. And that is as true of a life as it is of anything else: you are not stuck with the life you were handed — you are the creator of the one you're becoming.

So the practice is simple. See it — picture the person and the life you intend, clearly, in your own words. Believe it — hold it as true, not as a distant wish. And feel it — attach the joy and gratitude of the thing already done. See it, believe it, and visualize it with real emotion, and you have the oldest recipe there is for becoming who you intend to be.
