Best Practices

How to get the most out of Cosmic Intentions.

Writing Powerful Intentions
✦
Always start with "I AM"
Not "I want to be" or "I will be." I AM is present tense. It tells your subconscious mind this is already true — and your behavior follows.
🎯
Be specific, not vague
Weak: "I AM successful." Strong: "I AM a financially free entrepreneur earning $20k/month doing work I love." The brain needs a target to aim at.
💡
3–5 intentions is the sweet spot
Too many dilutes your focus. Too few limits your vision. Pick the areas of life that matter most right now: health, wealth, relationships, purpose.

The identity shift principle: Your results are always a reflection of who you believe you are. When you repeatedly state "I AM," you are programming a new identity — and identity drives behaviour automatically.

Using the AI Tools
📋
Fill your Pegboard first
The Setup page is the engine. The more detail you put in — your mission, values, obstacles, why — the more personalised and powerful every AI output will be.
🔄
Regenerate until it resonates
Not every AI output will hit perfectly. Use the regenerate button. Tweak your BTS context. Your best affirmations are the ones that give you a physical reaction.
✍️
Add your own words too
Use "Your Words" fields in each intention card. Your own language — the phrases you use naturally — often lands deeper than AI-generated text. Mix both.
Visualization Practice
🌅
Morning is the golden window
The first 20 minutes after waking, your brain is in a theta state — highly receptive to suggestion. Run your 3-Min Manifest before checking your phone.
🌌
Use the 10-Min Viz for deep sessions
Use headphones. Find a quiet space. Close your eyes during the countdown. Let the scenes wash over you. The 10-minute session is designed for full immersion — not multitasking.
📅
Consistency beats intensity
3 minutes every morning beats 30 minutes once a week. Your subconscious is reprogrammed through repetition. Show up daily — even when you don't feel like it.

The science: Visualization activates the same neural pathways as actually doing the thing. Elite athletes have used this for decades. You are training your brain to expect success — and to recognise it when it arrives.

Suggested Daily Routine
☀️
Morning (5–10 min)
Run the 3-Min Manifest. Read your I AM statements aloud. Review your #1 priority for the day.
🌙
Evening (5 min)
Write one win from the day. Read your intentions. Ask: "Did I act like this person today?" — then sleep on it.
📆
Weekly (15 min)
Run a 10-Min Viz. Update your Pegboard if anything has shifted. Regenerate your AI Library if your intentions have evolved.
