## Money & growth

**ARPU — Average Revenue Per User.**
All your subscription money in a month ÷ number of paying subscribers.
CI example: **$13.70** — blended, because monthly payers (~$12.99) and annual
payers (who work out to less per month) average together.
Answers: *"what does one subscriber put in my pocket each month?"*

**Margin (Gross Margin, "GM") — what's left after the cost of delivering the product.**
Out of $13.70, the AI voice + servers cost ~$1.50, leaving ~$12.20 = **~89% margin.**
High margin is why software beats a coffee shop (which keeps ~15¢ on the dollar).

**Churn — the % of subscribers who cancel each month.**
**7% churn** = 7 of every 100 leave monthly, so you must replace them just to stand
still. The single most important number in the business — full bucket vs. leaky bucket.

**Conversion rate — the % who take the next step.**
- **Visitor→trial 2.5%** = of 100 site visitors, ~2–3 start a free trial.
- **Trial→paid 45%** = of 100 who try it, ~45 become paying (high because a card is
  required to start the trial, which filters out tire-kickers).

**MRR — Monthly Recurring Revenue.** Predictable subscription income per month.
1,000 subs × $13.70 = **$13,700 MRR.**

**ARR — Annual Recurring Revenue.** MRR × 12. $13,700 × 12 ≈ **$164K ARR.**
Buyers value the business as a multiple of ARR.

**LTV — Lifetime Value.** Total money one customer gives before they churn.
At 7% churn the average customer stays ~14 months → LTV ≈ **$196** (~$176 after costs).

**CAC — Customer Acquisition Cost.** Marketing spend to get ONE paying customer.
CI target: **≤ $45.**

**LTV:CAC ratio.** LTV ÷ CAC. CI ≈ **$176 ÷ $45 = 3.9:1.** Above 3:1 is healthy —
you make ~$4 for every $1 spent acquiring.

**Payback period.** Months of subscription needed to earn back the CAC. CI ≈ **3.7 months.**

## Business & legal

**MoR — Merchant of Record.** A company (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy) that becomes the
*official seller*, handling taxes and payment liability so you don't. Shifts risk off you.

**ToS — Terms of Service.** The rules users agree to; where the liability cap and
arbitration clause live.

**LLC — Limited Liability Company.** A legal entity separating *business* money from
*personal* money, so a business lawsuit can't reach your house.

**EIN — Employer Identification Number.** The US tax ID number an LLC needs.

**FTC — Federal Trade Commission.** US consumer-protection regulator; punishes false
promises ("guaranteed results"). Reason CI sells *the ritual*, never *the outcome*.

**COPPA — Children's Online Privacy Protection Act.** US law on collecting data from
kids under 13 — why a "kids theme" carries risk.

**GDPR — General Data Protection Regulation.** Europe's strict privacy law; applies the
moment you have EU users.

**ICP — Ideal Customer Profile.** The one person you build and market for. CI's is
"Manifestation Melissa," 28–45, ~70% female.

## Tech

**API — Application Programming Interface.** A way for two programs to talk (e.g., the
app asking OpenAI for text).

**TTS — Text-To-Speech.** Turning written words into spoken voice (the Mind Loop).

**RLS — Row-Level Security.** A database rule so User A can't read User B's data.

**PWA — Progressive Web App.** A website that installs like a phone app.

**VA — Virtual Assistant.** A remote hire for support/admin work.

**Cache — a saved copy kept nearby so you don't redo expensive work.** E.g., saving
generated voice audio so you don't pay to regenerate it.
